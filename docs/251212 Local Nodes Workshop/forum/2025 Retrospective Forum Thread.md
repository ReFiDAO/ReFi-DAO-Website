## Thread Content

Hello ReFi Local Node Leaders and Community! 👋

As we approach the end of 2025, we're opening this space for our network to reflect on the year and share thoughts on where we're headed in 2026.

This thread comes on the heels of our **Local Nodes End-of-Year Support Workshop** (December 12), where we brought together node leaders from Colombia, Argentina, Spain, Mexico, France, the Netherlands, Miami, and beyond. The full workshop summary is posted below as the first reply.

Our intention here is to surface reflections from Local Node leaders and other network members — so that your insights can inform how we co-develop the network for 2026.

Over January, Monty and I will be working to translate these collective visions into actionable plans — gradually involving other ReFi DAO Global contributors, Local Node leaders, and wider community members. We look forward to your input! ✨

---

### We Want to Hear From You 💬

Whether you're an active Local Node leader, just getting started, or thinking about forming a node, we'd love your reflections on three areas:

**1. Key Highlights in 2025**
- What wins or breakthroughs stood out for your node or personal endeavors?
- What developments excited you across ReFi DAO, other local nodes, or the wider network?

**2. Key Challenges in 2025**
- What obstacles did you face locally?
- What gaps or friction did you notice at the network level?

**3. Visions for 2026**
- What do you want to achieve with your node or in your local context?
- What would you like to see from ReFi DAO and the network as a whole?

---

### Some Prompts to Get You Started

- What support would be most valuable in 2026?
- What would make onboarding easier for new nodes?
- What resources do you wish existed?
- How can the ReFi DAO network better serve local communities?
- What cross-node collaborations would you be excited about?
- Ideas for 2026 priorities?

# PART 2: First Reply - Workshop Summary

## December 12 Workshop Recap 📋

Here's what we covered in the workshop:

---

### Updated Onboarding Guide + Resources 📚

Over the past months, we've been focused on making the Local Node experience more streamlined and supportive:

- **🔄 Simplified onboarding process**: A new form-based application flow with clearer step-by-step guidance from initial interest to full activation.
- **🎨 Enhanced resources**: Updated Local Node resources for practical execution, including the [Local Node Toolkit v1.5](https://www.notion.so/refi-dao/Local-Node-Toolkit-v1-5-2aebf304370a81c5ba33d3794285d322), the [Local Node Canvas](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1220731037056160873) for strategic planning, and expanded brand/design templates.
- **💰 Funding pools active**: Local Nodes can access the Kickstarter Pool (up to 500 USDGLO), managed through [ReFi DAO's Garden](https://app.gardens.fund/gardens/42220/0x4f604735c1cf31399c6e711d5962b2b3e0225ad3/0x13e71c56c5b048e9b8b6a9dbb4a4f346b5dab986).
- **🌐 Website template (alpha)**: A free, self-hosted Local Node website template built on Quartz—already live for [ReFi Barcelona](https://refibcn.cat/) and [Regenerant Catalunya](https://regenerant.cat/), with more nodes currently testing it.

**→ Full onboarding guide:** [Starting a ReFi Local Node - Onboarding Guide](https://refi-dao.notion.site/local-node-onboarding)
